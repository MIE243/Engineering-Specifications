# 2. Detailed Requirements
Functions, objectives, and constraints of the project

### Demonstration

| Name                                 | Description                                                                                                                               | Target                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              | Reason                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    | Evidence at concept stage                                                                                                               |
| ------------------------------------ | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **D1 — Differential Function**       | **Must** allow differential action to be demonstrated at the front, rear, and centre positions of the drivetrain.                         | Differential behaviour shall be demonstrated at all three positions.                                                                                                                                                                                                | Core lesson: students should understand what a differential does and where differential action may occur in a drivetrain.                                                                                                                                 | Differential kinematics, drivetrain layout, and CAD operating configurations.                                                           |
| **D2 — Observable Speed Difference** | **Must** allow students to deliberately create and visually distinguish a different-speed condition between the outputs.                  | At least one student-controlled different-speed condition shall be available. Demonstration wheel speed should be approximately ≤ 60 rpm, and the faster and slower outputs shall be distinguishable by the unaided eye from the intended student viewing position. | Students must be able to directly observe and compare the output speeds in order to understand differential behaviour. A wheel rotating at or below 60 rpm completes at most one revolution per second, providing a conservative visual rate for the demonstration [1]. | Wheel-speed and drivetrain-ratio calculations; visible/high-contrast wheel markers; CAD motion study; planned visual observation check. |
| **D3 — Locked-Axle Comparison**      | **Must** demonstrate what occurs when the two wheels on one axle are forced to rotate at the same speed while the vehicle follows a turn. | Tyre scrub, slip, increased turning resistance, or drivetrain binding shall be observable at the demonstration.                                                                                                                                                     | Shows why an axle differential is required instead of only showing how the differential mechanism operates.                                                                                                                                               | Inner/outer wheel path-length calculation, locked and differential CAD configurations, and turning-geometry analysis.                   |
